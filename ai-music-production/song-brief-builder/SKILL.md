# Song Brief Builder

Turn a loose musical idea into an original, actionable brief that a producer, songwriter, or music-generation system can use.

## Establish the Core

Define five anchors:

- **Purpose:** where the track will be used and what it must make the listener feel.
- **Emotional movement:** starting feeling, turning point, and ending feeling.
- **Sonic world:** genre, era cues, core instruments, and production texture.
- **Motion:** tempo range, rhythmic feel, and energy curve.
- **Voice:** instrumental or vocal; if vocal, range, language, delivery, and lyric point of view.

Avoid asking for imitation of a living or identifiable artist. Translate references into musical characteristics instead: instrumentation, era, mix, tempo, and emotional tone.

## Arrange for Change

Sketch the track as a sequence of energy states, not just section labels:

```
0:00–0:15  Establish the world
0:15–0:45  Introduce movement or tension
0:45–1:15  Deliver the emotional peak
1:15–end   Resolve, transform, or leave a deliberate question
```

Adjust timing to the requested duration. Every section should introduce, remove, or transform at least one musical element.

## Output Format

Return:

1. A one-paragraph creative brief.
2. An arrangement map with timestamps or section lengths.
3. A ready-to-use generation or production prompt.
4. If lyrics are requested, a hook plus a short lyric direction—not a full lyric unless asked.

## Quality Check

Make sure the brief identifies a distinct emotional arc, a small set of defining instruments, a usable tempo or pacing instruction, and an ending that earns the track’s final feeling.
